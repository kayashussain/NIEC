# International Student Recruitment (ISR)

ISR is a recruitment portal that any education consultancy can use: the student sheet, student cards, messages, university Fact File, partners and agents, and a Dashboard. The name of the consultancy is a setting. This first installation is for Nepal International Educational Consultancy.
One login for everyone; each person sees only what their account allows.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Student Details** (`student-details/`) | The sheet of students by intake (the page is called **All Students** in the menu), with live co-editing, the Dashboard, the Users and Activity pages (Admin), and the **Fact File** (one page per university: fees, entry requirements, English tests, courses, intakes and the application process, searchable by course; the list is in two parts, **Universities** and **Pathway colleges**, with no mixed "All" list, the owner, 17 Nov 2026). |
| **Today** (`today.html`, `today-page.js`) | The first screen after login for every account, kept quiet on purpose: a greeting, then **Your tasks** (a team) or **Tasks you have given** (Admin and the Manager), **Today** and **Upcoming** (a team's tasks; for Admin the tasks the team gave them), **You gave** and, for Admin and the Manager, **Given by others**; each says who gave it to whom ("SK Kayas Hussain → Application Team"), is ticked done right there and has its own comments; a red dot marks what is new for the reader (a comment from the other side, a task just given to them), with a red badge on Today in the menu on every page until it is read; then one **Needs attention** list, a line per kind of work with how many students wait (inquiries to follow up, applications to submit, mock interviews to schedule, offers waiting for the deposit, CAS received with the visa not lodged, ...), opened with a click to show who. No number tiles and no buttons (those are on the Dashboard and All Students). Only the students the account may see are shown. |
| **My dashboard** (`my-dashboard.html`, `my-dashboard-page.js`) | Each team's own Dashboard (Application, Mock, Follow-up, each branch): four figures in the team's own words, the team's open tasks, **Needs attention** (the same list as on Today: what waits for the team, each kind with its count and the first students; the owner, 15 Nov 2026), a person in two teams with no own team chosen sees only those teams' figures and stages, **This week** against the week before, **Where the students are** by stage, **Coming up** in the next 7 days and **Good news** of the last 30 days. The Application, Mock and Follow-up teams' figures count the whole current intake (numbers only); names are only of the students the team sees. A team that opens /dashboard gets this page; Admin and the Manager keep the full Dashboard. |
| **Data to fix** (`data-fix.html`, `data-fix-page.js`, `/api/data-to-fix`) | Every gap in the students a person sees, on one page, grouped by what is missing (the owner, 17 Nov 2026): a language test chosen as "Test Date" with no day, a step on the sheet with no day (applied, offer), an applied student with no university or course, the date of birth, passport number and address (from Application Stage on), a phone number, an email, the source, and the details that do not add up. Each gap is filled in where it is listed and saved straight to All Students or the student card; the rest opens the card. Each team sees only its own kinds of gap (Application: the application and passport details; Mock: the test day, the offer day and the phone; Follow-up: phone, email and source); Admin, the Manager and a branch see all of them, the Manager to look at only. A student who already has the visa is asked only for the sheet's own steps. **Work through them** opens the first student of a group with Previous and Next on the card (and the [ and ] keys). |
| **Student card, fewer clicks** (`student-page.js`) | One tap to Call, WhatsApp, Email or copy the number; a **Next step** panel that records the next day right on the card (the language test day, the application, the offer) or opens the passport details; and, when the card was opened from a list, "3 of 12" with Previous and Next (the owner, 17 Nov 2026). |
| **Weekly summary** (`weekly.html`, `weekly-page.js`, `/api/weekly`) | For Admin and the Manager, from the Dashboard's **Weekly summary** button (the owner, 17 Nov 2026): the last 7 days against the 7 before, every intake: new students (by their first inquiry day), applications, offers, deposits, CAS, visas lodged and received, each on the day written on All Students; who received a visa or an offer, paid or applied; the teams' work (tasks given and done, notes, messages); tasks done by team and new students by source; and what is still open. **Earlier week** goes back a week at a time; **Print** prints it without the menu. |
| **Data tidy-up** (`data-tidy.js`) | At every start of the server: an intake's dates written like 7/15/2026 are written as 2026-07-15, only when that intake's own dates prove which comes first; "Chitwan" as Receiving Partner becomes "Chitwan Branch" where that name is used. Each change is written in Activity ("Tidied the data"). |
| **Calendar and dates** (`shared/ui.js`) | Every date box opens an Apple-style calendar on a computer (the phone's own date wheel on a phone), and every date is written "25 Aug 2026" (the day first, the month in words). |
| **Student card** (`student.html`, `student-page.js`, `cards.js`, `portal-api.js`) | Opens from a double-click on a student's name, email, number, university, course or row number (a click on a date cell opens its calendar), and from the Fact File. Two tabs (the owner, 17 Nov 2026): **Notes** (plain notes about the student, no kinds to choose; the newest first; the history also shows every task and every message sent from Follow-up) and **University options** (the options chosen for the student, then a study level (Foundation, International Year 1, Top-up, Undergraduate, Pre-Masters, Postgraduate (MBA too), Masters by Research or PhD), a course name search and remarks, kept on the card; each course found has **Add** and **Open**, its university's page in the Fact File). There is no Send a message: **Call**, **WhatsApp**, **Email** and **Copy number** are one tap under the name. **WhatsApp** on a computer opens the student's chat **on the person's own phone** (the owner, 17 Nov 2026; `push.js`, `phone-link.js`, `sw.js`, `wa.html`): the phone is connected once, in two steps: on the computer, the menu (your name), **Connect my phone** shows a code; point the phone's camera at it and tap **Turn on** on the phone (no login on the phone; the code works 30 minutes, once; an iPhone shows one extra step, Add to Home Screen, because Apple allows notifications only from there); after that a click on WhatsApp makes the phone show a notification, and one tap on it opens the chat in WhatsApp on the phone. This is standard Web Push (encrypted for that phone, signed by the server, made with Node's own crypto, no outside service or account; the server's keys and the connected phones are in `push-keys.json` and `push-devices.json` in the data folder, never in git). With no phone connected, the click shows how to connect one and offers to open the chat on the computer this time. On a phone, WhatsApp opens at once. **+ Add student** asks, in this order: where the student starts (the stage); the student (first and last name, email, WhatsApp number, date of birth, passport number, address as in the passport, nationality; the passport details are needed from Application Stage on, optional at Inquiry); the student source (for Social Media: the platform, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp, then Organic or Advert, and for an advert the campaign from Events and marketing or its name under Other); and optional remarks. A student already in ISR (same phone or email) is never added twice: the form names them and offers Open their card or **Fill in what is missing**; the same phone under a clearly different first name (a brother or sister on the family phone) also offers **Add anyway (a different person)**, and an event list never links such a line on its own (it is shown as Check); which fills only blank details, tags the source when none was recorded, and keeps a different email or phone in a note (an application's email is never replaced). The card's top panel reads Email, Date of birth (with the age when the student applied), Passport number, Address, WhatsApp, University, Course, Application submitted and Offer received; the nationality shows for a student from outside Nepal; they change with Edit details; an empty passport detail says **Missing** in orange, and from Application Stage on the student is listed on Today under **Passport details missing** (Admin, the Manager and the Application Team). Remarks and Student Source are not columns of the sheet any more (their data stays in the file).

## Run it on your computer

```bash
npm start            # the site on http://localhost:8080 (Incentive at /incentive, set INCENTIVE_PASSWORD to open it)
```

The first time, the server writes a one-time setup code to `first-admin-code.txt` in its data folder (and prints it
in the log). Open `/login`, enter it, and create the owner (Admin) account. Needs Node 18 or newer; there is nothing
to install.

## Try it on your computer first

```bash
node scripts/local-demo.js     # made-up students and data, at http://localhost:8080 (log in as owner, password Demo-Password-2026)
```

Nothing real is in it and nothing leaves the computer (no email is sent). `--keep` starts it again on the demo folder as you left it.
Details and the other demo logins are at the top of `scripts/local-demo.js`.

## Tests

```bash
npm test
```

The rules, data and security checks run anywhere. The browser tests (`test/ui.test.js`: log in, edit, delete, search,
team and Manager rules, Fact File, Incentive, accessibility) run where Playwright and Chromium are installed
(`npm i -D playwright axe-core`) and skip themselves otherwise.

## Deploying and running it

- `DEPLOY.md`: Render or any server with `deploy.sh`.

## Claude Strategy Advisor connector (read-only)

Lets a Claude Project read the live sheet (every tab, row and column), the Dashboard numbers and the Fact File. It cannot
change any student or the Fact File. The only thing it can write is the Advisor's own notebook (`advisor-memory/` in the data
folder: preferences, how NIEC works, lessons; notes with emails or phone numbers are refused, and every save keeps a backup). It is off until you set `NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN` (a random secret of 32 or more characters) in the host
settings and restart. Then add this address in claude.ai (Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector):

```
https://<your-site>/advisor-mcp/<NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN>
```

Anyone with that address can read all students, so keep it private. To cut access off, remove or change the variable and restart.
Make a secret with `node -e "console.log(require('crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('hex'))"`.

### The rest of the portal through the same connector

The same connector also reads **everything else the portal stores** (the owner, 17 Nov 2026), read-only and live:
`get_tasks` (every task with its student, team, tag, who gave it, when, whether done, and all comments), `get_student_file`
(one student's sheet row and whole card: profile, notes, course options, messages, tasks), `get_portal_areas` (the list of areas)
and `get_portal_data` (one area: student cards, partner universities, B2B partners, agents, planning, users, mail settings,
templates, source options, course links and watch, the Navitas reading, exchange rates, the activity record, the
message log). **Passwords, password hashes, tokens, keys, login sessions and phone push keys are never shown.** Nothing can be
changed through it. Claude may need the connector refreshed in claude.ai to list the new tools.

### Incentive questions through the same connector

The connector above also answers questions about the **Incentive page**, with no second connector and no new address. After the
Student Details app restarts, Claude gets four more read-only tools: `get_incentive_overview`, `get_incentives` (every visa-issued
student with their **University Partner** (the agent), tuition fee, university commission and the incentives entered),
`get_payment_summary` (Payment Summary and Company Earnings of an intake) and `summarize_incentives_by` (totals by University
Partner, university or intake). They cannot change anything. Claude may need the connector refreshed in claude.ai to list them.

The Student Details server reads the Incentive page's saved figures (`commissions.json`, `advances.json`, `exchange-rate-cache.json`)
straight from the Incentive data folder. It finds that folder next to its own data folder (`.../niec_data/counselor-incentive`, the
layout in `CPANEL_DEPLOY.md` and `deploy.sh`), or set `COUNSELOR_DATA_DIR` on the Student Details app to the same value the
Incentive page uses. If the folder cannot be found, the incentive tools say so instead of showing zeros. Because it is one connector,
**the one secret opens both the students and the commissions**: keep the address private. Both connectors' shared code is in
`shared/mcp-core.js`.

## Daily backups and restore (Admin only)

Every day after 6 p.m. (office time, `APP_TIMEZONE`, default Asia/Kathmandu) the Student Details server saves one compressed
backup of the students, the Fact File and the Partner Universities in `daily-backups/` in the data folder, and deletes copies
older than 30 days. If the host has put the server to sleep, the copy is made at the first request instead. Accounts and passwords
are never in a backup. Admin restores from **Activity → Restore data** (students, Fact File and Partner Universities each on
their own); a restore first saves the current data as a "before restore" backup. **View** opens the real Student Details, Fact File and Partner Universities pages on that backup (`?backup=ID`, read only, nothing saved or polled), with a bar to switch between them and restore. Uploaded agreement files are not in the backup.

## See your real data on your own computer
