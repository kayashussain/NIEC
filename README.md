# International Student Recruitment (ISR)

ISR is a recruitment portal that any education consultancy can use: the student sheet, student cards, messages, university Fact File, partners and agents, and a Dashboard. The name of the consultancy is a setting. This first installation is for Nepal International Educational Consultancy.
One login for everyone; each person sees only what their account allows.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Student Details** (`student-details/`) | The sheet of students by intake (the page is called **All Students** in the menu), with live co-editing, the Dashboard, the Users and Activity pages (Admin), and the **Fact File** (one page per university: fees, entry requirements, English tests, courses, intakes and the application process, searchable by course). |
| **Today** (`today.html`, `today-page.js`) | The first screen after login for every account, kept quiet on purpose: a greeting, then **Your tasks** (a team) or **Tasks you have given** (Admin and the Manager), **Today** and **Upcoming** (a team's tasks; for Admin the tasks the team gave them), **You gave** and, for Admin and the Manager, **Given by others**; each says who gave it to whom ("SK Kayas Hussain → Application Team"), is ticked done right there and has its own comments; a red dot marks what is new for the reader (a comment from the other side, a task just given to them), with a red badge on Today in the menu on every page until it is read; then one **Needs attention** list, a line per kind of work with how many students wait (inquiries to follow up, applications to submit, mock interviews to schedule, offers waiting for the deposit, CAS received with the visa not lodged, ...), opened with a click to show who. No number tiles and no buttons (those are on the Dashboard and All Students). Only the students the account may see are shown. |
| **My dashboard** (`my-dashboard.html`, `my-dashboard-page.js`) | Each team's own Dashboard (Application, Mock, Follow-up, each branch): four figures in the team's own words, the team's open tasks, **Needs attention** (the same list as on Today: what waits for the team, each kind with its count and the first students; the owner, 15 Nov 2026), a person in two teams with no own team chosen sees only those teams' figures and stages, **This week** against the week before, **Where the students are** by stage, **Coming up** in the next 7 days and **Good news** of the last 30 days. The Application, Mock and Follow-up teams' figures count the whole current intake (numbers only); names are only of the students the team sees. A team that opens /dashboard gets this page; Admin and the Manager keep the full Dashboard. |
| **Calendar and dates** (`shared/ui.js`) | Every date box opens an Apple-style calendar on a computer (the phone's own date wheel on a phone), and every date is written "25 Aug 2026" (the day first, the month in words). |
| **Student card** (`student.html`, `student-page.js`, `cards.js`, `portal-api.js`) | Opens from a double-click on a student's name, email, number, university, course or row number (a click on a date cell opens its calendar), and from the Fact File. Three tabs: **Notes** (plain notes about the student, no kinds to choose; the newest first), **University options** (the options chosen for the student, then a study level (Foundation, International Year 1, Top-up, Undergraduate, Pre-Masters, Postgraduate (MBA too), Masters by Research or PhD), a course name search and remarks, kept on the card; each course found has **Add** and **Open**, its university's page in the Fact File) and **Send a message** (six templates, each opening on the full-page composer). **+ Add student** asks, in this order: where the student starts (the stage); the student (first and last name, email, WhatsApp number, date of birth, passport number, address as in the passport, nationality; the passport details are needed from Application Stage on, optional at Inquiry); the student source (for Social Media: the platform, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp, then Organic or Advert, and for an advert the campaign from Events and marketing or its name under Other); and optional remarks. A student already in ISR (same phone or email) is never added twice: the form names them and offers Open their card or **Fill in what is missing**; the same phone under a clearly different first name (a brother or sister on the family phone) also offers **Add anyway (a different person)**, and an event list never links such a line on its own (it is shown as Check); which fills only blank details, tags the source when none was recorded, and keeps a different email or phone in a note (an application's email is never replaced). The card's top panel reads Email, Date of birth (with the age when the student applied), Passport number, Address, WhatsApp, University, Course, Application submitted and Offer received; the nationality shows for a student from outside Nepal; they change with Edit details; an empty passport detail says **Missing** in orange, and from Application Stage on the student is listed on Today under **Passport details missing** (Admin, the Manager and the Application Team). Remarks and Student Source are not columns of the sheet any more (their data stays in the file).
| **Messages** (`messages.js`, `templates.html`, `compose.html`) | Admin's emails end with the office signature picture (sent inside the email); a team member's end with their own picture, or their name as text until they upload one. A consultancy puts its own `email-signature.png` in the data folder (`STUDENT_DETAILS_DATA_DIR`) and that picture is used; `student-details/email-signature.png` is only the first installation's starting picture. Six templates, and **Other** to write your own words: university options and documents, documents needed, application submitted, offer received, mock interview, deferred. Each opens on a full page (`/compose`): the email at full size, editable words, files to attach (read in the browser, sent once and never stored on the server), then Send, Open in my email program, Copy or WhatsApp (`wa.me`). The options email can carry a minimum cash deposit column. Email goes from the office mailbox set up in `mail.json`. Admin edits the templates.
| **University options** (`course-index.js`, `fact-finder.js`) | Every course of the Fact File as one searchable list. On the student card: a study level (Foundation, International Year 1, Top-up, Undergraduate, Pre-Masters, Postgraduate (MBA too), Masters by Research or PhD), a course name with a **Search** button (Enter works too) and remarks, kept on the card; each course found has **Add** and **Open** (its university's page in the Fact File). On a Fact File page, **Send to a student** picks courses and a student for one email. The earlier assistant that judged a typed description against the entry rules was removed (14 Nov 2026), as the owner asked for a plain search. |
| **Course pages and the weekly check** (`university-websites.js`, `course-finder.js`, `course-watch.js`) | The official website of each university is listed in `university-websites.js` (or written in the Fact File). Only those websites are searched or opened. A course with no page written down is searched for; every course page is opened about once a week (a slice every six hours, the first ten minutes after the server starts; `COURSE_WATCH=off` stops it). A page that is gone, no longer about the course, or whose fees or intake months changed becomes a **warning** on the Fact File (a line on the Fact File page, a list on the university's page) for a person to confirm. The check never edits the Fact File itself. Results are in `course-watch.json` in the data folder. |
| **My email and WhatsApp** (`user-mail.js`, `my-email.html`, `my-email-page.js`) | Each person sets up their own mailbox (address and password, checked with a test email, the password kept encrypted on the server) so the emails they send to students go from their own address, signed with their own name and team. A team member without one cannot email; Admin falls back to the office mailbox. WhatsApp always opens in the person's own WhatsApp. |
| **Follow-up** (`bulk.html`, `bulk-page.js`) | One message to many students at once, by email or WhatsApp, each with their own name and details. The students are chosen by intake, stage, **Source** (Social Media, Visitor, a branch, an agent, or exactly which platform or campaign) and name. |
| **Portal logins and signatures** (`partner-access.js`, `user-mail.js`, `my-email-page.js`) | The username and password of a university's or a partner's application portal are shown only to Admin and the Application Team (on Partner Universities, B2B Partners and Agents; the student card shows no portal link or login, the owner, 15 Nov 2026); everyone else sees the portal link. A Mock Team person counts as Application Team only when the Application Team is their own team (the owner, 15 Nov 2026), so the Mock Team never sees a portal password or the commission; where a login is held back the page shows a dotted Password whose eye answers "You do not have access". Each team member sets up their own email and uploads a signature picture (PNG or JPG, at most 1 MB) on **My email** (reached from **Message templates**, "Your email and signature"); their emails end with that picture, Admin's with the office signature. There is no typed signature form and no Company tab (the owner, 15 Nov 2026): Message templates has the templates and one tick box, whether the office mailbox gets a copy of every email. The company name used across ISR stays a stored setting. The account menu holds only Log out. |
| **Growth, ups and downs, and where students live** (`dashboard-trends.js`, `nepal-map.js`, `/api/trends`) | On the Dashboard, in a balanced order (a chart, then a pair of plain cards, then a chart): the headline figures; **Ups and downs over time** (a chart like a stock market's: one line each for inquiries, applications, offers, payments, CAS and visas, by day, week or month, on the day each happened, a lead list's own inquiry days included; tap a name to show or hide its line; each line's peak marked; a crosshair reads every line; events and campaigns marked; a source filter narrows every line to the students of one source, where a student from an event, a campaign or a paid advert counts under Events and marketing; a few sentences on the last 4 weeks against the 4 before); **How students found us** and the visa target; **Business growth by intake** (two plain bar charts, students and visas in each intake with the numbers printed, one sentence on the last closed intake, and a table with each figure's change on the intake before and the **visa rate**, visas for every 100 students; the newest marked In progress); **Where students live in Nepal** (a map with a dot per district, read from the address in the passport; the students from outside Nepal by nationality; the age when they applied); then partners and the student journey, and universities. A round **up** button at the bottom right of every page goes back to the top once a page is scrolled. The map's shapes are from geoJSON-Nepal (MIT License). |
| **Tasks** (`cards.js`, `today-page.js`, `student-page.js`) | Admin and the Manager press **Assign a task** on a student card: choose the team (Application, Mock, Follow-up or a branch); every team gives a task to every other team, every branch and Admin, never to its own team, and reassigns by the same rule (the owner, 15 Nov 2026: the Mock Team gives the Application Team, the Follow-up Team, the branches and Admin; the server refuses anything else), and a branch gives its own students' tasks to the Application or Mock Team; the Manager, every team and every branch can also give Admin a task (Admin is named with their name and the signature's title, for example "SK Kayas Hussain (Head of Recruitment)"); whoever gave a task sees it under **Tasks you have given** on Today and can remove it, and write what to do (no date to do it by, the owner, 14 Nov 2026). The team sees it under **Your tasks** on Today and ticks it complete (or not complete again). Each task has its own **updates**: the team asks for what it needs (a student's email, a document) or reports progress, with or without ticking, and whoever gave the task answers; both see it on Today and on the student card. Notes on the card are about the student; the talk about a piece of work is in its task; while it is open, and for 7 days after, the team can open that student even if the student is not at its stage. Admin and the Manager see every task they gave under **Tasks you have given**, with who completed it. **Reassign**: the team a task is with, whoever gave it, Admin or the Manager reassigns it to another team (or to Admin) in the same task, with a reason and next step; it is a line in the updates ("Reassigned: Mock Team → Application Team"), the route shows every team it went through ("SK → Mock Team → Application Team"), and the teams it was with still see it (under **Reassigned by your team**) and post updates; only the team it is with ticks it. Reassign and Remove sit together at the task's top right. Each task has a **tag** saying what it is about (Application, Mock interview, Payment, Documents, NOC, Medical, Visa and CAS or Other), **chosen by hand** in Assign a task (it cannot be given without one) and Edit the task, never set by the system (the owner, 15 Nov 2026); a task given before then shows the tag worked out from its words until someone chooses one; pressing the tag on a task changes it. There are no priorities (the owner, 15 Nov 2026). Both are changed by pressing the task's pills. Each list shows something new first, then the one waiting longest. On Today the tasks show one list at a time, chosen with a switch beside the Tasks title: **Today** (this person's or team's tasks for today), **Upcoming** (theirs moved to a later day, under a heading per day), **You gave**, and where there are any **Given by others** and **Reassigned** (the owner, 15 Nov 2026), each with its number and a red dot for something new; a search box finds a student's tasks by name (or any word of a task or its tag), and a row of pills shows one kind at a time, and each list shows its first five tasks until Show all is pressed (the owner, 15 Nov 2026). **Notifications**, behind a bell at the top right of Today (a red count shows what is new; a press opens them, Escape or a click outside closes them), keep every task given, update, change, reassignment and completion (whoever gave a task hears when it is done) of the last 30 days, read or not: the new ones first under New (a blue dot), the rest under Earlier. A press goes straight to that task with its updates open and marks it read; Mark all as read clears the New ones. A task counts as read only when its updates are opened, an update is written or its notification is pressed (never by touching or scrolling past it). A task shows its updates and a "..." menu (Edit the task, Reassign, Remove) at its right. **Edit the task**: whoever gave it, Admin or the Manager changes its words and tag in place (no need to remove it and give it again); it shows "Edited" and the team gets a notification. **Marking a task done is asked once more** ("Mark as done? ... Yes, it is done"), so it is never ticked by a slip; the same window **asks what the task's tag needs and fills in the student by itself** (the owner, 15 Nov 2026: the team writes it once, not in the notes, the task and the sheet): **Application**, submitted on (a calendar) and to which university: the date goes into Application submitted when it is empty, and a second application (a backup option) is kept in the notes with its own date; **NOC**, Pending (the account made) or Submitted, and the NOC number: into NOC and NOC Number; **Mock interview**, the day it is set for: into the next empty Mock Schedule, and the team gets "Write the feedback of mock interview N" waiting under Upcoming for that day; **Medical**, Booked (the day and the hospital or clinic: Medical Report says Booked and becomes Received by itself on that day, checked every hour, unless booked again for another day, when the new day counts), Report received, or Not booked yet (a booking that fell through is cleared); **Payment**, paid on and how much: Payment Date when empty and the amount in the next empty Tuition Fee Deposit; **Visa and CAS**, the step (CAS requested, CAS received, visa lodged, VFS attended, visa received) and its day: into its column, the stage moving on with it (CAS Issued, Visa Lodge, Visa Issued), never back; any other tag, one line on what was done. Each answer is also one plain note on the student (`portal-api.js` finishPlan, medicalSweep); the sheet's Remarks shows the latest such line, only when Remarks is empty or still holds ISR's own last line (a remark a person typed is never overwritten); every application is also kept in the student's University options with its own date ("Applied 15 Nov 2026"), so a backup application shows beside the first; an application is never dated after an offer already on the sheet, and a NOC never goes back from Submitted to Pending. The Application Team may fill in the Medical Report on All Students; NOC offers Pending and Submitted, Medical Report Booked and Received. **Notes and history** on the student card: the notes and every task event (given, each update, reassigned, moved, done) in one timeline, newest first, with Everything / Notes / Tasks pills; nothing is typed twice, and a removed task with work on it is kept as a note. An open task shows how long it has waited from the third day ("Open 5 days"). **Today and Upcoming** (the owner, 15 Nov 2026): the team, whoever gave a task, Admin or the Manager can **Move to another day** (the "..." menu, a calendar); the task waits under **Upcoming** (its own switch for the person's tasks; in You gave and the other lists a grey "Upcoming (N)" line under today's tasks, by day; a kind with only later tasks says "2 later") and is back under Today on that day by itself; the move is written in its updates and whoever gave it is told. Each task is its own soft card on the page with room around it: a round circle to tick, the student (a blue dot when something is new) and the stage, the task, then one grey line with the tag, who gave it and **who has it by name** ("Owner → Parvati", from the people whose own team it is; everyone in the team when nobody has it as their own team, so a task never reaches nobody), and the day; on every open task the latest two updates and one rectangular box to write an update, Post inside it at the right (Ctrl+Enter posts too), a red "N new" at its top right. **Every open task shows** (no "Show all"); **a done task leaves Today** (it stays on the student's card, and its notification opens the card); the later ones are only under the Upcoming switch (in You gave and the other lists, under a "Later days" line). Today carries no explanatory sentences, only the titles, the numbers and the tasks. On Today each task starts with the student (name in bold, as people say it, and the stage), then the task under it; a notification names the student first too. Today says how many tasks are for today and upcoming, and whose tasks come to the person ("Tasks of the Application Team"; a person in two teams with no own team chosen is told that every team's tasks come until Admin chooses it). **The Board** (Admin and the Manager, the owner, 15 Nov 2026), the only view of the tasks for them (no List): a column per team ("For you" first) with its open tasks only (done work is in the students' notes), its count, who has its tasks and how many wait 3+ days; each task a small tile (the student, the task, its tag, how long it has waited, its number of updates and the latest update in one line; a blue edge when something is new); a press opens the whole task in a window (tick, updates, Edit, Reassign, Open the student card); the kind pills and the search narrow it; the page is up to 1600px wide; Admin always has a "For you" column first ("Nothing for you right now." when empty). **Assign a task** says exactly who gets it ("Goes to Parvati"), from the people whose own team it is. **Their own team** (Users, the owner, 15 Nov 2026): a person ticked in more than one team (for example the Application Team, given the Mock Team's students too so nobody is missed) must have the team they belong to chosen; their tasks, their Today and their dashboard come from that team only, the account menu names it, and the other teams are extra access (they still see and change those students). In the list of people their own team is a solid chip and the others "+ Mock Team"; a person in two or more teams with none chosen shows "Own team not chosen" and gets every team's tasks until it is chosen; until then their Today asks them "Which team do you belong to?" (one button per team, they choose it themselves) and Admin's and the Manager's Today name who has not chosen yet. Today reads, for everyone: the greeting, the day at a glance (what is open for today and upcoming, how many wait 3 days or more, and how many different students wait, each counted once even when in two lists; for Admin and the Manager the whole team's tasks), the tasks, then Needs attention; Needs attention also lists students whose **Student source is not recorded** (Admin, the Manager, the Application and Follow-up teams), so every student counts in the Dashboard. Kept on the student's card (in the daily backups); every change is in Activity. |
| **Events and marketing** (`events.html`, `events-page.js`, `planning.js`) | Admin adds, the Manager reads. Every event or piece of marketing (a fair, a seminar, a Facebook advert, a signboard ...) has a name, where it ran (Social media, Our office, A college or school, Another venue, Newspaper/radio/TV, Online ads, Other; older entries are read from their old type), the days it ran, its cost (typed in rupees, US dollars or pounds; dollars and pounds are turned into rupees at the day's rate, from the same rate site as the Incentive page, and every total is in rupees) and a note. Each is a tag: choose it as the Student source on a student (one by one with Add student, or many with **Add many students**, offered inside Add student), and it shows on the student card with its dates. Which students came from an event or a campaign is settled by the list Admin uploads for it (open it on Events and marketing, Excel or CSV, up to 1,000 lines): every line is shown for review before anything is saved; new students go into the intake they asked for (a column such as "Intended UK Study Intake: January 2027" puts them in Jan Intake 2027), else the chosen intake, at Inquiry, with the source Social Media for a campaign on social media and Events for anything else, tagged with it; their names are tidied (stray symbols, capitals), a Nepal number is kept as 98XXXXXXXX, each column is read by what its heading means, so any list works (a Facebook lead export, an event's sign-up sheet, a campaign report): the inquiry date (12 November 2026, 12/11/2026, 2026-11-12 or an Excel date) and its time become the first inquiry day and time, shown on the card in the Source panel; the programme becomes the study level; the programme, the intake asked for, comments and anything else about the student go into the first note; what is about the list, not the student (the inquiry or serial number, ids, links such as an inbox link, and the platform, campaign, ad and form names) is left out; a student already on the sheet (same phone, matched on its last 10 digits, or email, in any intake) and in no event is tagged with it instead of being added twice (the earlier source is kept in the remarks and a note goes on the card); a student already tagged with another event is left as it is and listed; a line with no name, or with neither a phone nor an email, is skipped; the same phone under another first name (a brother or sister) is shown as Check and not linked. A list that knows more about students already in ISR is never stopped (shown as **Update**): a blank email or phone is filled in (a different one goes into a note, the sheet's is kept), the first inquiry day becomes the earliest day known (so a lead added before the list had its days gets its real day), a blank study level is filled in, the time comes with the earlier day, and the comments or other details about the student go into a note ("More from the list of ..."), only the parts the card does not already say; a note an earlier version wrote by copying every column ("From the updated list of ...") is tidied away by the next upload; the same list uploaded again with nothing new changes nothing. Re-uploading is safe (the owner, 14 Nov 2026): the same person twice in one file is **Joined** into one line with the details of both; a lead that fits two different students in ISR (the phone one's, the email another's, or two students sharing a number) is **Check** and changes neither; a student removed from the sheet earlier is **Removed before** and never added back by a list (each card keeps the phone and email its list gave, and a card stays when its row is deleted); a cancelled, withdrawn or held student keeps their stage, a list only adds details. To fix a list without deleting the event, **Remove the enquiries this list added** (Admin, on the event's page) shows who goes and who stays, and removes only the students the list itself added who are still at Inquiry and that nobody has worked on (no university, application, task, option, message or note from the team); students worked on, and students in ISR before the list, stay. A copy of everything is saved first (Admin, Restore), and the corrected list can be uploaded straight after: its leads are added as new. A campaign can still be picked under Social Media or Events when a student is added (Events needs the event). Each event opens as its own view with its figures (cost per student, cost per visa) and its students with their latest note; a click opens the student card. On All Students, **Source** shows only the students of one source (Social Media, Visitor, Reference, Language Classes, Events and marketing, Agents, Branches) or exactly which (a platform, an event or campaign, a branch, an agent), for every account and the students it sees. The name, email, phone, university, course and partner columns have their own filters too. The page warns how many social media students are on no campaign's list. An updated, longer list for the same event or campaign can be uploaded at any time: the people already tagged are recognised ("already linked", or "Update" when the list has something new about them) and the new ones are added; anyone tagged earlier who is not on the new list is named in an orange note in the review and left as they are (never removed). Excel files are read by ISR itself (`student-file.js`), so a sheet formatted as a table (as Meta exports leads) reads too; the Excel library is only a fallback. |

## Claude Strategy Advisor connector (read-only)

Lets a Claude Project read the live sheet (every tab, row and column), the Dashboard numbers and the Fact File. It cannot
change any student or the Fact File. The only thing it can write is the Advisor's own notebook (`advisor-memory/` in the data
folder: preferences, how NIEC works, lessons; notes with emails or phone numbers are refused, and every save keeps a backup). It is off until you set `NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN` (a random secret of 32 or more characters) in the host
settings and restart. Then add this address in claude.ai (Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector):

```
https://<your-site>/advisor-mcp/<NIEC_ADVISOR_TOKEN>
```

Anyone with that address can read all students, so keep it private. To cut access off, remove or change the variable and restart.
Make a secret with `node -e "console.log(require('crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('hex'))"`.

### Incentive questions through the same connector

The connector above also answers questions about the **Incentive page**, with no second connector and no new address. After the
Student Details app restarts, Claude gets four more read-only tools: `get_incentive_overview`, `get_incentives` (every visa-issued
student with their **University Partner** (the agent), tuition fee, university commission and the incentives entered),
`get_payment_summary` (Payment Summary and Company Earnings of an intake) and `summarize_incentives_by` (totals by University
Partner, university or intake). They cannot change anything. Claude may need the connector refreshed in claude.ai to list them.

The Student Details server reads the Incentive page's saved figures (`commissions.json`, `advances.json`, `exchange-rate-cache.json`)
straight from the Incentive data folder. It finds that folder next to its own data folder (`.../niec_data/counselor-incentive`, the
layout in `CPANEL_DEPLOY.md` and `deploy.sh`), or set `COUNSELOR_DATA_DIR` on the Student Details app to the same value the
Incentive page uses. If the folder cannot be found, the incentive tools say so instead of showing zeros. Because it is one connector,
**the one secret opens both the students and the commissions**: keep the address private. Both connectors' shared code is in
`shared/mcp-core.js`.

## Daily backups and restore (Admin only)

Every day after 6 p.m. (office time, `APP_TIMEZONE`, default Asia/Kathmandu) the Student Details server saves one compressed
backup of the students, the Fact File and the Partner Universities in `daily-backups/` in the data folder, and deletes copies
older than 30 days. If the host has put the server to sleep, the copy is made at the first request instead. Accounts and passwords
are never in a backup. Admin restores from **Activity → Restore data** (students, Fact File and Partner Universities each on
their own); a restore first saves the current data as a "before restore" backup. **View** opens the real Student Details, Fact File and Partner Universities pages on that backup (`?backup=ID`, read only, nothing saved or polled), with a bar to switch between them and restore. Uploaded agreement files are not in the backup.

## See your real data on your own computer

The students you see in `scripts/local-demo.js` are made up. To look at your real data without touching the live server: download a backup from the
live server (Admin, Restore, or the daily backup), then run `node scripts/local-live.js <the downloaded file>` and open http://localhost:8080. It opens straight into the portal as the owner with no login page, and only on that computer (add `--login` for the normal login page). The copy
is private to your computer (folder `live-data/`, never in git) and changes made there never reach the live server. Delete `live-data/` when you finish.
